Why this hike is worthwhile

Explore a mountain lake and its wetlands on an approximately one-mile loop at Brighton. Boardwalk, forest, and wildflowers offer plenty to notice at a relaxed pace. This guide covers the lake loop only, excluding connecting trails to other lakes.

Know Before You Go

Preparation

Bring water, layers, sun protection, and insect repellent. Stay on the designated loop and leave wildflowers for others to enjoy.

Seasonal hazards

Moose are wild and unpredictable: do not approach them. Mosquitoes can be troublesome. Check weather, snow, ice, and footing before choosing the outing.

Seasons and weather

Summer is the planning filter supported by the source’s July–August wildflower guidance. This does not establish suitable conditions on any date or exclude other seasons.

At roughly 8,760 feet, plan conservatively for mountain weather. Check the forecast and current snow/ice conditions; bring layers and turn back when conditions exceed your group’s abilities.

Fees

Fees and parking/pass requirements are not stated in this source. Confirm with the Forest Service before visiting.

Access and restrictions

Confirm current closures, parking, pet/watershed restrictions, and accessibility with the land manager. This source is not a current access report.
