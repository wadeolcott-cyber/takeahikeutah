# First Steps source-review log

Reviewed October 21, 2026 (America/Denver). Source review records available route information, **not current access**. Official pages can be updated or cached; visitors must check conditions before travel. No third-party photographs were downloaded or reused. The provided Bryce hero remains unchanged; trail images are labeled original illustrative placeholders.

## Verification scope

- Silver Lake Loop: Forest Service requests reached the host but returned an upstream HTTP 403 access-denied response. Route statistics, surface, duration, seasons, fees, and trailhead details remain unverified. This record is pending; its supplied location is not represented as independently reviewed.
- Bridal Veil Falls: Utah County legacy park links redirect to `https://publicworks.utahcounty.gov`; the running proxy still denied that destination after the reported settings save at the time of this review. Construction was flagged by the project owner, not independently verified. Keep the manual-access warning. Do not infer a usable approach or free parking.
- Bryce Sunset–Sunrise: NPS publishes 1.0 mile round trip, 40 ft gain, paved concrete, and approximately one hour (the activity metadata says 20–60 minutes). The accessibility paragraph has an inconsistent metric conversion; use the main route description. This is only the paved segment, not the entire Rim Trail. Winter preparation differs.
- Jenny’s Canyon: Utah State Parks publishes 0.5 mile round trip, 20 minutes, and easy rating. Sand/uneven rock are described on the hiking page; slopes and steps on the official brochure. Numeric cumulative elevation gain is not published in the reviewed sources. Do not transfer Johnson Canyon’s separate seasonal closure to Jenny’s.
- Ladyfinger Point: official map labels 0.25 mile one-way, doubled here for a 0.5-mile return outing. Official sources reviewed do not specify this route’s gain, surface, or walking time. The displayed 20–40 minute estimate is editorial. General island wildlife, insect, and access rules are source-backed.
- Sand Dune Arch: NPS lists 0.4 mile round trip, 15–30 minutes, and 0 ft **elevation change**. Its mobility page describes bedrock, deep sand, and a boulder in a narrow passage. Cumulative gain is not separately published. Broken Arch route variants are excluded. Spring/fall planning follows NPS climate guidance.
- Capitol Reef Sunset Point: NPS lists 0.4 mile **one-way**, doubled to 0.8 mile for the return trip; **less than 50 ft elevation change** is not an exact cumulative gain. The 30–45 minute walking-time estimate is editorial and excludes sunset viewing. NPS lists the sunset activity year-round; snow, ice, heat, and storms can still make it unsuitable. A generic `/places/sunset-point.htm` page refers to Mammoth Cave, not Capitol Reef, and was deliberately excluded. Fee text follows the reviewed NPS activity/general fee pages rather than promising a free viewpoint.
- Huber Grove: park pages and brochure confirm the visitor-center trail network, historic farmhouse/creamery, aspen grove, and old orchard. Route length, numeric gain, walking time, and exact trail surface were not found in the official material. Keep those fields unknown and ask park staff to confirm the route variant. Spring/summer/fall tags are editorial dry-condition planning, not an official route-specific season designation.
